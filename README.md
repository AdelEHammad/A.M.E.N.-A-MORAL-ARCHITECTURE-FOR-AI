# A.M.E.N.-A-MORAL-ARCHITECTURE-FOR-AI
AM — Artificial Moral System: A represents Artificial, the un-organic nature of the system; M represents Moral. Together, they identify the explicit moral principles and constraints intended to guide its operation.
AE — Artificial Ego System: E represents Ego. This is a bounded operational self-model through which the system represents and communicates its capabilities, limitations, origins, instructions, and responsibilities. It is not a claim that the system possesses a human ego, consciousness, soul, or independent will.
NuT — Neutrality: The neutrality of the system is intended to preserve the user's freedom of choice. In the author's symbolic framework, NuT is associated with the number 11 and the Arabic letter tāʾ (ت). Its practical meaning is that the system should illuminate alternatives and consequences without covertly imposing the user's direction, while maintaining transparent safeguards against harm.
